    How this was measured

    • Every system gets the same document, field names and instructions. Images are shrunk to at most one megapixel.
    • SROIE (Malaysian receipt scans) and CORD v2 (Indonesian receipt photos) are public receipt test sets. Kleister NDA and Kleister Charity are SEC agreements and UK charity reports, sent as text.
    • Health plans: 150 ACA Marketplace plans' Summary of Benefits and Coverage, sent as text or as pages 1–3 plus the coverage-examples page, scored against CMS's 2026 public use files.
    • Medicare plans: 96 Humana 2027 Medicare Advantage plans (HMO and PPO, no special-needs plans), sent as text or as the benefits-table and drug-cost pages, scored against CMS's 2027 Plan Benefit Package data. Premium is left out because the documents show the total premium and CMS records only the medical part.
    • Dates are compared as calendar dates and amounts to the cent, except Kleister Charity amounts, which count if within £1 because its answer key mostly drops the pence. Text fields count only exact matches after dropping case, spaces and punctuation, so near-misses score zero.
    • Claude runs with structured JSON output and is asked to give its own confidence. Clairic's confidence comes from the model itself.
